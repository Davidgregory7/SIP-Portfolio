Hardware-independent proof of concept

Daily support, without losing human context.

MemoryLens simulates how smart-display reminders could help a person with early-stage memory loss while giving an authorized caregiver a clear, limited activity record.

No smart glasses required

This MVP runs entirely in the browser. It demonstrates the planned workflow and does not claim direct Meta Ray-Ban Display compatibility.

Included in MVP

Reminder scheduling, four response states, unanswered-state simulation, caregiver activity log, responsive layout, and persistent browser data.

Future integration

Compatible wearable hardware, voice/gesture input, authenticated caregiver accounts, encrypted cloud synchronization, and usability testing.

Safety boundary

MemoryLens is a support concept—not a diagnostic tool, medical-device replacement, or confirmation that medication was actually taken.
